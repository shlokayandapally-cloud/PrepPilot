Personalized Tips

Simple, actionable ways to make your next answer stronger.

Your interview toolkit
🧭
Structure your answer

For experience-based questions, try STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep the focus on what you personally did.

🔎
Be specific, not generic

Swap “I’m hardworking” for a short example that demonstrates it. Details make your answer memorable.

🪞
Reflect on the lesson

Finish stories with what changed, what you learned, or what you would do differently next time.

⏳
Practise out loud

Aim for 60–90 seconds for most answers. Record yourself if you can, then check clarity and pace.

A quick answer checklist

Before you finish an answer, ask yourself:

✓ Did I answer the actual question?
✓ Did I include a real example?
✓ Did I explain my own contribution?
✓ Did I share a result or lesson?
✓ Was my answer clear and concise?
